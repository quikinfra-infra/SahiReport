<h2>2. Information We Collect</h2>

<h3>2.1 Information you provide</h3>
<ul>

<h3>2.2 Information collected automatically</h3>
<ul>

      moment of submission, as part of the App&rsquo;s core tamper-proof reporting function.</li>
  <li><strong>Timestamps:</strong> the date and time each report and photo is submitted.</li>
  <li><strong>Device tokens:</strong> a push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to
      deliver notifications to project owners.</li>

      reliably and securely.</li>
</ul>
